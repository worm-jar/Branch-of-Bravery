# 🌺BRANCH OF BRAVERY

Branch of Bravery is an Action 2D Sidescroller Action Game where you play as a villain in a fairytale inspired world. It is a short demo that I made as my end of year 1 project.

## 💻SOFTWARE USED

- > Unity/C#
- > Krita
- > Cubase
## 🕹️GAMEPLAY

The combat of this game is something that sets it apart, and is the main focus of the game. You consume health to deal damage and regenerate by striking enemies with a light attack that deals very little damage. This forces the player to be aggressive in moderation, as you need to get close up and personal to regenerate health, while also dodging and playing reactively to not take damage.

Inspired by the likes of Hollow Knight and Dark Souls, timing, positioning, and resource management are the difference between life and death.

The player can jump, dash, and perform 2 different types of attacks.

## 📖LORE/STORYTELLING

In Branch of Bravery, you are sent as an envoy of a god to kill an anomaly located at the top of Mount Lunaria. This anomaly took part of this god's consciousness and built a sort of resistance against this selfish god and a refuge for those who were meant to die without a trace.

At the base of the mountain, blocking the path to the peak, is Brave Little Annabeth; A little girl, who can barely carry a sword, and who wants nothing but to protect her family. Branch of Bravery is then a small part of this full game, where you reach and defeat Brave Little Annabeth.

The player is a representation of death with a scythe and a top hat. They went through a couple of redesigns before finding the sweet spot. The final boss, Brave Little Annabeth, with her golden, shiny hair, is designed to stand out from the dark shades of gray and purple of the environment and enemies.

In the story of the game, the anomaly at the top of Mount Lunaria manages his loneliness and boredom by writing poetry, which is how you learn about the lore and story of the game. It is then fitting that the UI is made to look like handwriting on paper.

## ✏️THE PROCESS OF MAKING BRANCH OF BRAVERY

Everything started with wanting to make a boss fight, **Brave Little Annabeth**, for my end of year project. Indeed, the story behind it all was something I came up with a long time ago. 
I started with the player movement, then did the art and sprite-work for the characters and their environment.

However, after having finished and somewhat polished to fight, I realized I had way more time to expand the game by doing some level design, adding enemies, adding cutscenes, and, most importantly, creating a proper tutorial.

After that, I used the feedback gained from playtesting to polish and adjust parts of the game. 

Given the short amount of time, there are elements that need more polish and could be improved:

- > Some of the platforming was not calibrated properly
- > Some found the game to be overly difficult and frustrating
- > The tutorial can be confusing to some
- > Other less important details

Overall though, I am quite happy with how it all turned out.
